Privacy Policy for Match-a-Doodle

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

Match-a-Doodle ("we", "our", or "the Game") is a handcrafted match-3 puzzle game. We respect player privacy and are committed to transparency regarding how data is handled.

1. Data We Collect and How It Is Used

Match-a-Doodle is built with privacy-by-design. We do not require account registration to play, and core gameplay does not harvest personal details.

  • Local Gameplay Data: Level progression, stars earned, high scores, coins, and booster counts are stored strictly on your device using local storage or device keychain. We do not upload your gameplay to external tracking servers.
  • Optional Player Tag: If you choose to set a custom Doodler nickname or backup email, it is retained locally on your device for game display and local progress restore.
  • Crash & Performance Diagnostics: We may utilize anonymous technical logs (such as OS version or crash logs) solely to diagnose performance bugs on low-end hardware and improve frame rates.

2. Advertising and Monetization

Match-a-Doodle offers 100% optional, non-forced rewarded video ads (e.g. to earn extra moves upon completing a challenging level). We partner with Google AdMob to deliver sponsored video ads.

  • Google AdMob may process non-sensitive device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or IP address) solely for fraud detection, frequency capping, and ad delivery.
  • For information on how Google manages advertising data, please visit Google's Advertising Policies.

3. Children's Privacy

Match-a-Doodle does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The game contains family-friendly, non-violent cartoon sketchbook puzzles.

4. In-App Purchases & Virtual Items

Any optional in-game purchases of coins or booster packs are processed securely through the Google Play Store billing service. We never collect or store your payment card numbers, bank credentials, or billing addresses.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your game data is stored directly on your device, you have complete control over it. You can clear your game history, stars, and coins at any time via the in-game Settings menu ("Reset Progress") or by clearing the app data in your Android system settings.
